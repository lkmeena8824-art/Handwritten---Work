1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how HandWrite Work ("we", "us", "our") handles personal information collected through this website and through the application form on it. We operate a work-from-home handwriting work programme. We are a privately operated business and are not a government department, agency or scheme.

For any privacy question, you can reach us at support@yourdomain.com or through WhatsApp support during 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM (Mon–Sat).

2. Information we collect

We only collect what we actually need. In practice, that is the following.

WhatExamplesWhy we need it
Information you give us in the application form Full name, mobile number, email address (optional), city or district, education level, hours you wish to work, previous experience, your optional message To assess your application, contact you about it and match you to suitable work
Information you send us later Handwriting samples, completed page photographs, and messages you send us on WhatsApp, Telegram or email To verify quality, process your work and provide support
Technical information Basic, non-identifying data your browser sends (such as the page you visited). If analytics or advertising tools are enabled on this site, they may set cookies — see section 6 To keep the website working, understand how it is used and improve it

What we never collect on this website: we do not ask for bank account numbers, card details, UPI PINs, passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs) or any payment credentials through this website or its forms. If any page or message ever asks you for those in our name, treat it as a fraud attempt and report it to us immediately.

3. How we use your information

  • To review and process your application to join the programme.
  • To contact you about your application, your work, quality checks and payouts.
  • To send you programme updates, work availability and instructions through Telegram, WhatsApp, SMS, telephone or email.
  • To provide support and answer your questions.
  • To keep records required for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and dispute resolution.
  • To improve our website, our instructions and the programme generally.

We do not use your information for any purpose that is unrelated to the programme described on this website, and we do not use automated decision-making to make decisions about you.

4. Our lawful basis for processing

Depending on where you live, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Your consent — given when you tick the consent box and submit the application form, or when you message us voluntarily. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract — where we need to contact or pay you in connection with work you have agreed to complete.
  • Legitimate interests — running and protecting our business, preventing fraud and misuse, and keeping the website secure, always balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligations — where we are required to keep records under applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and, where relevant, the UK GDPR.

5. Sharing your information

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers who help us run the programme — for example form-processing tools, hosting providers, messaging platforms and accountants. They may only use your data on our instructions.
  • Platforms you contact us on — if you message us on WhatsApp or Telegram, that platform processes your data under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements — where we are required by law, court order or a lawful request from a public authority to disclose information.
  • Business transfer — if the programme or business is transferred or merged, information may transfer with it, and we would tell you if that happened.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

This website is a simple static website and does not require cookies to work. However:

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when the page loads.
  • Advertising and analytics tools (for example the Meta Pixel or a website analytics tool) may be added to measure how our advertisements perform. If we enable them, they may set cookies and process data such as your IP address, device and pages viewed. Where required, we will ask for your consent first.
  • Application drafts may be stored in your own browser's local storage so that your details are not lost if the page is refreshed. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you submit the form.

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; the website will continue to work.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Unsuccessful applications — deleted or anonymised within 12 months.
  • Applications of people who join the programme — kept while they are active with us, and for up to 3 years afterwards where needed for accounting, tax and dispute purposes.
  • Work submissions and handwriting samples — kept while needed for quality checking and payout records, then deleted.
  • Anything you ask us to delete — deleted on request, unless we are legally required to keep it.

8. How we protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Access is limited to authorised team members who need the data to do their work.
  • Devices holding applicant data are protected with passcodes or biometric locks.
  • Transmissions to and from this website are secured with HTTPS.
  • Application records are stored in access-controlled systems, not on public computers or shared spreadsheets.
  • Team members are instructed never to ask applicants for money, OTPs, PINs or passwords.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay if a breach affects your personal data. You can read more on our Data Security page.

9. Your rights and how to use them

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your data — request erasure and we will remove it unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including opting out of work updates.
  • Object or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing.
  • Complain to us, or to your local data protection authority, if you are unhappy with how we have handled your data.

To exercise any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp support or email support@yourdomain.com with your full name and the mobile number you applied with. We aim to respond within 30 days. You can also use our data deletion request process.

10. Age requirement

The programme is for adults only. You must be 18 years or older to apply, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we hold data belonging to a person under 18, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a minor has submitted an application, please tell us so we can remove it.

11. Third-party links and platforms

This website links to third-party platforms, including Telegram and WhatsApp. Once you leave our website, that platform's own privacy policy applies. We do not control those platforms and are not responsible for how they handle your data — please read their policies before joining a channel or sending a message.

12. International transfers

Our team operates from India. Some service providers (for example hosting, form-processing or messaging providers) may store data on servers located outside your country. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure your data continues to receive an appropriate level of protection.

13. Marketing communications

We contact applicants and members about the programme — work availability, instructions, support and payment matters. We do not sell your contact details to any third party for their own marketing. You can ask us to stop sending non-essential updates at any time, and we will stop within a reasonable period. Please note that we may still contact you about work you have already accepted, or where we are legally required to do so.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example if we add a new tool or change how we contact applicants. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always show when it last changed. Continued use of this website after an update means you accept the revised policy.

15. How to contact us

  • WhatsApp support: use the Support button on any page — for questions and privacy requests.
  • Email: support@yourdomain.com
  • Telegram: our channel — for applications, work updates and general information.
  • Support hours: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM (Mon–Sat)
