How It Works

From joining to getting paid — five simple steps

No interview rounds, no office visits, no training fee. Everything happens from your phone.

1

Join the Telegram channel

Tap “Join Telegram Channel”. All work updates and task releases are posted there — it is the main channel we use.

2

Submit a handwriting sample

Write a few lines on plain paper, photograph it and send it. This is only to confirm your handwriting is neat and readable.

3

Receive your work pack

Approved applicants get the assignment material and clear instructions — what to write, on which pages, and by when.

4

Write 3–10 hours a day

You decide your own hours each day. Finish the pages, photograph them and submit them for quality checking.

Get paid for your hours

Once your submission passes the quality check, payment is processed to your PhonePe, Paytm, UPI ID or bank account.

The Work

Simple writing work — no degree or experience required

You are given the content. You write it out neatly and submit photographs of the finished pages.

  • Notebook and register writing — copying given content neatly, paid per completed page and per hour worked.
  • Form and document filling — writing details into printed forms and documents.
  • Assignment and letter writing — longer pieces where presentation matters most.
  • Work any hours you wish — 3, 4, 5 or 10 hours a day. There is no minimum target and no penalty for a lighter day.
Requirements

What you need to get started

Very little — most people already have everything on this list.

  • Age 18 or above — applicants must be adults.
  • Neat, legible handwriting — clarity matters far more than speed.
  • A smartphone and internet — that is all you need to receive and submit work.
  • Notebook and pen — plus a well-lit spot to take clear photographs.
  • A payout account — PhonePe, Paytm, UPI ID or bank account in your own name.

FAQ

Questions applicants ask us most

Still unsure? The same answers are on WhatsApp support if you would rather ask a person.

Is there any registration fee or deposit?

No. Applying is completely free. We never charge a registration fee, joining fee, material fee, training fee or security deposit. If anybody asks you for money in our name, do not pay it — report it to us on WhatsApp straight away.

How many hours can I work each day?

You choose. Applicants commonly work 3, 4, 5 or 10 hours a day — whatever fits around studies, housework or another job. There is no minimum target, and a shorter day is never penalised. Because payouts are calculated hourly and per completed page, your income goes up the more hours you put in.

How and when do I get paid?

Once your submitted work passes the quality check, payment is processed to the account you nominate — PhonePe, Paytm, UPI ID or a bank account in your own name. Payout timings and per-hour rates are confirmed with you privately before you start, because they vary with the type of assignment.

How much can I earn?

Earnings depend entirely on your own work — how many hours you complete and how neatly the pages are written. We do not promise a fixed monthly income or a guaranteed salary, and we would rather be honest about that than quote a figure that may not suit you.

Is this a government job or a government scheme?

No. This is a private work programme operated by a registered company. We are not affiliated with any government department or scheme. Government recruitment never happens over Telegram or WhatsApp, and we will never claim otherwise.

Do I need a laptop or a computer?

No. A smartphone with internet is enough to receive your work pack, photograph your pages and submit them. A notebook and pen are the only other essentials — a laptop is optional.

Can students, homemakers and retired people apply?

Yes, absolutely. The work is designed for part-time hours from home. The only eligibility requirement is that you are 18 or older and your handwriting is neat and legible.

What happens after I submit the application form?

Your details reach our team, who contact you about the next step. The quickest route is to also join the Telegram channel, because that is where work updates are posted — applications are actioned in the order they arrive.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. We collect only what is needed to process your application, store it with limited authorised access, never sell it, and delete it whenever you ask. Full details are in our Privacy Policy and Data Security pages.

How do I contact support?

Use the WhatsApp button anywhere on this page, or email us. Support hours are 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM (Mon–Sat). WhatsApp is for questions and help only — applications and work are handled on Telegram.
