Requirements

FAQ

Aapke sawal — hamare jawab

Aam taur par pooche jaane wale sawal. Aur kuch poochna ho to WhatsApp message kar dein.

Kya is kaam ke liye koi fee lagti hai?

Nahi. Joining bilkul free hai. Koi registration fee, material fee ya security amount nahi li jaati. Agar koi aapse paise maange to turant WhatsApp support par report karein.

Kitni earning ho sakti hai?

Earning aapke complete kiye gaye pages/kaam par depend karti hai — jitna achha aur time par kaam, utni achi earning. Kisi bhi fixed income ki guarantee nahi di jaati.

Payment kab aur kaise milega?

Verify kiya gaya kaam weekly process hota hai. Payment UPI ya bank transfer ke through bheja jaata hai.

Mobile se kaam ho sakta hai?

Haan, kaam samajhne aur submit karne ke liye mobile kaafi hai. Writing ke liye notebook + pen chahiye hoga. Behtar speed ke liye laptop optional hai.

Student ya housewife hoon — kar sakti/ sakti hoon?

Bilkul! Ye kaam part-time ke liye design kiya gaya hai. Students, housewives aur retired log sab kar sakte hain — bas age 18+ honi chahiye.

Ye kya government job hai?

Nahi. Ye ek private handwriting work-from-home opportunity hai. Ye kisi government department se affiliated nahi hai.

Technical problem ho to kya karein?

WhatsApp support button dabayein — hamari team technical aur practical dono tarah ki help ke liye available hai.
