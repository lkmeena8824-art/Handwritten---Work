  • You must have neat, legible handwriting and access to a notebook, pen and a smartphone or computer with internet.

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance. We accept applicants based on availability of work, handwriting quality and our internal checks.

  • You may not pass your assignment to another person or have somebody else complete your pages on your behalf.
  • Completed work must be submitted in the format and within the timeframes we specify.
  • Submissions that do not meet our quality standards may be returned for correction, or may not be payable. We will explain the reason.
