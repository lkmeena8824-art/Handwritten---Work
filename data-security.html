Data Security

How we protect your information, and how to get it deleted. Last updated: 28 September 2026.

Our promise in one line: we collect the minimum, we share nothing for marketing, and we delete your data whenever you ask. We will never ask you for money, an OTP, a PIN, a password or your bank details.

1. What data we hold

We keep the following, and nothing more than we need:

  • Application details — your name, mobile number, optional email address, city, education level, hours you wish to work and any optional note you write to us.
  • Work records — the assignments issued to you, the pages you submit, quality-check results and payout records.
  • Support messages — conversations you have with our team on WhatsApp, Telegram or email.

We do not hold your bank account number, card details, UPI PIN, passwords or one-time passwords collected through this website. Payout details are exchanged privately once you are accepted, through channels we control.

2. Who can see it

  • Access is restricted to authorised team members whose work requires it — for example the person handling applications or quality checks.
  • Access is granted by role, and is removed when a team member leaves.
  • We do not allow any team member to copy applicant data into personal files, personal drives or personal messaging groups.
  • Accidental or excessive access is treated as a security incident and investigated.

3. Where and how it is stored

  • This website is served over HTTPS, so information submitted through the form is encrypted while travelling to us.
  • Application records are kept in access-controlled databases, spreadsheets or form platforms — not on shared or public computers.
  • Devices used by our team are protected with screen locks, passcodes or biometric locks.
  • We keep the website's software up to date to reduce the risk of known vulnerabilities.
  • If you start completing the form and refresh the page, a draft copy may be stored in your own browser's local storage. That copy stays on your device; you can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

4. Payments and financial safety

We will never ask you for: an OTP, a UPI PIN, a CVV, a card number, an ATM or net-banking password, a screenshot of your bank login, or any payment of any kind — no registration fee, deposit, material fee or training fee.

Nothing on this website collects payment details. If any page, message, call or video asks you for those things in our name, it is a fraud attempt. Stop the conversation, do not share anything, and report it to us immediately.

Payouts move in one direction only: from us to you, into the account or wallet you nominate. That happens after your work is checked and accepted.

5. Sharing with service providers

To run the programme, we may use trusted service providers — for example a form-processing tool, a hosting provider, a messaging platform or an accountant. They receive only the data they need, only for the purpose we have engaged them for, and they are not permitted to use it for anything else. We never sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers for their own marketing.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Unsuccessful applications are deleted or anonymised within 12 months.
  • Records for active members are kept while they work with us, and for up to 3 years after that where needed for accounting, tax or dispute purposes.
  • Support conversations are kept only as long as needed to help you and to keep a record of what was agreed.
  • Anything you ask us to delete is deleted, unless the law requires us to keep it (for example an accounting record after a payout).

7. Request deletion of your data

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you, at any time. It is free, and you do not have to give a reason.

  1. Message us on WhatsApp support or email support@yourdomain.com.
  2. Send the words "Delete my data" along with your full name and the mobile number you applied with.
  3. We will confirm we have received your request, and complete the deletion — normally within 7 working days, and no later than 30 days.
  4. If we are required to keep a record (for example a payout record for accounting), we will tell you exactly what we are keeping, why, and for how long.

Full details of your wider rights are in section 9 of our Privacy Policy.

8. If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will:

  • Contain it and stop further exposure as quickly as possible.
  • Notify the relevant data protection authority where the law requires it.
  • Tell you without undue delay if there is a risk to your rights or safety, and explain plainly what happened and what you should do.
  • Review what went wrong and change our process so it cannot happen the same way again.

9. Spotting fraud in our name

Sadly, dishonest people copy the names of genuine work programmes. Five warning signs:

  • They ask for money — a registration fee, deposit, kit fee or "refundable" charge. We never do.
  • They ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, CVV or bank password. We never do.
  • They contact you from a random personal number and refuse a video or voice call from our official channel.
  • They promise a fixed monthly salary regardless of the work done, or ask you to recruit other people for a commission.
  • They claim to be a government department. We never claim that — this is a private programme.

10. How you can help

  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords with anybody — including someone claiming to be from us.
  • Use a screen lock on your phone and keep it updated.
  • Keep your payout details private; send them only through the channel our team confirms to you.
  • Tell us straight away if you suspect fraud, in our name or in anyone else's.

11. Report a security concern

  • WhatsApp support: use the Support button on any page.
  • Email: support@yourdomain.com
  • Telegram: our channel
  • Support hours: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM (Mon–Sat)

If money has already been taken by somebody pretending to be us, please also report it to your bank and to your local cybercrime authority (in India, cybercrime.gov.in or helpline 1930).
